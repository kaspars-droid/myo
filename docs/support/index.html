Myo Calc — Support

For Myo Calc 1.2 on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Myo Calc is a line based calculator: you write on the left, the answers appear on the right, and the whole thing is a plain text file you own.

Getting in touch

Email: kaspars@cirsis.lv

Bugs and requests: github.com/kaspars-droid/myo/issues

Myo Calc is made by one person, so an answer may take a few days rather than a few minutes. If something is wrong, the useful things to say are: which device and which version of iOS or macOS, what you typed, what appeared beside it, and what you expected instead. A sheet is plain text, so pasting the lines themselves into the message is usually the fastest way to show the problem.

The first line names the sheet, which is why it is also the file's name. The bar along the bottom totals the whole sheet, and each currency is totalled on its own, since the app holds no exchange rates and will not invent one.

Answers are written the way your device writes numbers. If your region uses a comma for decimals, then you can type 12,50 as well as 12.50, and the answers come back in the same style.

Where your sheets are kept

A sheet is a plain text file ending in .myocalc. It opens in any text editor and stays readable without this app, which is the point of it.

The app keeps its sheets in a folder you choose. Until you choose one, it keeps them privately inside the app on the device. On iPhone and iPad the app also holds its own copy of that folder so sheets open instantly and still work with no signal.

The same sheets on more than one device

Point each device at the same folder in a service that syncs — iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, or anything else that appears as a folder. The app writes ordinary files, so the sync is the service's job rather than the app's.

One thing to know before you rely on it: there is no merging. If the same sheet is edited on two devices at once, the last write wins and the other version is gone. Editing one sheet in one place at a time is safe; editing it in two places at once is not.

If something looks wrong

  • A line has no answer. The app leaves a line alone when it cannot read it as a sum, so prose stays prose. Check for a stray word inside the figures.
  • The total looks too small. total only adds what was written since the last blank line, and a named figure such as rate = 0.21 is deliberately kept out of the totals.
  • A sheet is missing. Look in the folder itself with Files or Finder. The sheets are ordinary files, so if they are there, they are not lost, whatever the app is showing.
  • A cloud sheet did not arrive. Open the folder in the service's own app first and let it download, then reopen Myo Calc.

What you need to run it

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS or iPadOS 26.5 or later.
  • Mac: macOS 15 or later.

Privacy

Myo Calc collects nothing, has no accounts and makes no network connections. The full statement is at kaspars-droid.github.io/myo/privacy/.
