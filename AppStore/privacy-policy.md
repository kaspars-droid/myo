# Privacy Policy — Myo Calc

Last updated: 19 August 2026. Applies to Myo Calc 1.2 for iOS, iPadOS and
macOS, published by Kaspars Cirsis.

## The short version

Myo Calc collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising,
no tracking, and no servers of its own. It does not connect to the internet.
What you type stays on your device, in files you can see and delete yourself.

## What the app stores, and where

**Your sheets.** A sheet is a plain text file ending in `.myocalc`, saved in a
folder you choose the first time you use the app. Nothing about that file is
hidden from you: it opens in any text editor, and you can move, copy or delete
it without the app.

**A local copy of that folder.** Sheets often live in a cloud folder, which on
a phone is fetched on demand and may not arrive at all when offline. So the app
keeps its own copy inside its private storage on the device. That copy holds
the same sheets and nothing more. Deleting the app deletes it.

**Which folder you picked.** The app remembers your choice so it does not have
to ask again. On macOS this is stored as a security-scoped bookmark, which is
Apple's mechanism for a sandboxed app to reopen a folder you granted it. It
records a location on your own disk, not the contents of anything.

That is the complete list. There is no other file, database or setting in which
the app keeps anything about you.

## What the app does not do

- No personal information is requested, collected or stored — no name, email,
  address, phone number, or contacts.
- No account, sign-in or registration.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage measurement.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
- No tracking, and nothing shared with data brokers or any other company.
- No third-party SDKs or frameworks beyond Apple's own.
- No location, camera, microphone, photos, health or calendar access.
- No network requests. The macOS app ships without the entitlement that would
  let it make one at all.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is sold, shared, transferred, profiled or
used to build a profile of you, and there is nothing for us to disclose in
response to a request from anyone.

## If your sheet folder is a cloud folder

You may point the app at a folder that syncs — iCloud Drive, Google Drive,
Dropbox, or anything else that presents itself as a folder. If you do, that
service copies those files off the device according to your arrangement with
it, under its own privacy policy, not this one. The app simply reads and writes
files where you told it to; it has no connection to any such service, and no
way to send your sheets anywhere on its own.

## What Apple sees

Apple distributes the app and handles the purchase, if any. Apple may give us
aggregated, anonymous statistics — how many downloads, which countries, crash
counts — and, if you have opted into sharing analytics with app developers in
your device settings, anonymised diagnostic reports. None of this identifies
you, and none of it contains anything you typed into a sheet. Apple's handling
of your data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy at
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Children

The app is safe for use at any age. It collects no data from anyone, including
children under 13, and contains no advertising, no purchases and no links out.

## Your data, your rights

Since we hold nothing about you, there is nothing to request, correct, export
or ask us to erase — under the GDPR, the CCPA, or any comparable law. Your
sheets are yours: to delete them, delete the files, or delete the app, which
removes its local copy with it.

## Changes

If a future version of the app does something this policy does not describe,
this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at
the top will change to say so.
